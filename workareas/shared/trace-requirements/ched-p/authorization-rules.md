# CHED-P delegated-authority model

## Evidence scope

The corpus contains **no CHED-P/CVEDP delegated-authority notification journey**. The notification-level DoA observations were rendered on CHED-PP journeys using Plant-organisation fixtures and commodity code `06011010`; the shared organisation chooser, address isolation and authorisation-management observations are type-agnostic or Plant-fixture evidence. This model carries only that delegated-authority mechanism across to CHED-P, where the assigned organisation is a **POAO organisation**. It does not infer POAO-specific page copy, commodity behaviour or a CHED-P-only DoA route. See [`doa-findings/_discovery.md`](doa-findings/_discovery.md).

The terms `createdFor` and `assignedOrg` below describe the notification-owning organisation selected by the user. They are model names, not claims that those identifiers were visible UI labels.

## Rules

1. **An importer or member can act for their own organisation.** A user operating as the importer/member creates in their current organisation context, and that organisation becomes the notification owner. The direct baseline is the Plant-member journey catalogued as trace `6e71cf94f89d27e643a2c97d05b67b153700b499` in [`doa-findings/_discovery.md`](doa-findings/_discovery.md); the shared organisation context is rendered in trace `c90c9d4c32cdc6a7919ccadf9c0f039d3167fb25`, actions 18-21 ([finding](doa-findings/c90c9d4c32cdc6a7919ccadf9c0f039d3167fb25.json)).

2. **An authorised agent can act on behalf of a client organisation.** The agent first chooses “A different organisation” and then chooses a company from the represented set. Only a business associated with the agency and authorised for on-behalf-of action belongs in that set. Evidence: trace `253df9dcdf570dfa90715630fcd0b175ae37c695`, actions 10-12 ([finding](doa-findings/253df9dcdf570dfa90715630fcd0b175ae37c695.json)); trace `cc6cea8221f684d1090a9a3f0f15cf782b46de84`, action 20, renders the agency’s authorised companies ([finding](doa-findings/cc6cea8221f684d1090a9a3f0f15cf782b46de84.json)).

3. **B2B users can act within their B2B role and organisation scope; this does not make them notification owners.** B2B access remains permission-driven and must respect the notification’s assigned organisation and operational role. The corpus discovery identifies the generic B2B/user-permission traces as broad role-access evidence but not delegated-client creation evidence; therefore this rule must not be interpreted as blanket cross-organisation visibility. Evidence boundary: “Generic auth, permission, and organisation candidates” in [`doa-findings/_discovery.md`](doa-findings/_discovery.md), including user-permission traces `03bcfdad57a49f6c22cb6ead164e319bd57e9692`, `264cea65ad1d01d2e6dc86afcd5e6fb33a22fc02`, `44bbb6797617dbf47833fe1c240b360866f57e0b`, `ac636f6aa8bdb7b4a814773fa218e78414faa84a`, `f0a0c1227b1a517a128df949607f6d2ada26dbbf` and `fbb5948140ef4c2b1a494757fd848dae30696cc3`.

4. **The notification is owned by its assigned organisation, not necessarily by the creator’s employer.** Selecting the client sets `createdFor`/`assignedOrg` to that client. After submission, the card names the client as consignee and remains assigned to it even though the agent performed the work. Evidence: trace `253df9dcdf570dfa90715630fcd0b175ae37c695`, actions 94-95 and 99 ([finding](doa-findings/253df9dcdf570dfa90715630fcd0b175ae37c695.json)); trace `545412266caaec96b0aae12262fecfee3e099888`, action 94, separately retains Carol Clark as submitter and the client as responsible organisation ([finding](doa-findings/545412266caaec96b0aae12262fecfee3e099888.json)).

5. **An agent can change the assigned organisation before submission.** The owner choice supports the agent’s own agency or a represented client, and a draft can be changed from the client to the agency before submit. All organisation-derived defaults and final ownership must follow the final selection. Evidence: trace `253df9dcdf570dfa90715630fcd0b175ae37c695`, actions 10-12, renders the choice; trace `851759a01b1ff318ca2a82fb278f9c41b05e9616` is catalogued in [`doa-findings/_discovery.md`](doa-findings/_discovery.md) as “creates for Plant Org 1 → changes organisation to Plant Agency C before submitting.”

6. **Submitted on-behalf-of work is marked with the Trade Partner badge.** The badge is the visible ownership cue that the notification is client-assigned trade-partner work; the card also names the assigned client organisation. Evidence: trace `253df9dcdf570dfa90715630fcd0b175ae37c695`, actions 94-95 and 99 ([finding](doa-findings/253df9dcdf570dfa90715630fcd0b175ae37c695.json)); trace `b1f31ed70fbdb2f92f66488da5bda6f3e510a0c9`, actions 91-92 ([finding](doa-findings/b1f31ed70fbdb2f92f66488da5bda6f3e510a0c9.json)).

7. **Members of the assigned/creating organisation can see a submitted notification.** A client member in the client organisation context can find the agent-submitted notification and can View details, Copy as new and Amend. Evidence: trace `2c16ad659671044cd9b138be5a71b21c658af3b8`, actions 87 and 93-95 ([finding](doa-findings/2c16ad659671044cd9b138be5a71b21c658af3b8.json)). The same visibility direction is also catalogued for traces `065de8c56896cc607da83498efba9c11575a7438` and `e9b5e36b926ee3b7ac855c45cf8169dd38321ad4` in [`doa-findings/_discovery.md`](doa-findings/_discovery.md).

8. **A client-organisation member cannot see the agent’s draft before submission.** Draft visibility remains with the creator/agent; assignment alone does not publish an unfinished draft to other client members. After submission, rule 7 applies. Evidence: trace `b701cc405623fb2d2e2b2dedcf2bec3d10dfb954` is catalogued in [`doa-findings/_discovery.md`](doa-findings/_discovery.md) as “Carol Clark (agent for Plant Org 1) drafts → Isabel Irwin (Plant Org 1 member) cannot see it”; submitted visibility is confirmed by trace `2c16ad659671044cd9b138be5a71b21c658af3b8`, actions 87 and 93-95.

9. **The submitting agent retains visibility after submission.** The creator can find the submitted client-owned notification on their agent dashboard and receives View details, Copy as new and Amend controls. Agent visibility does not transfer ownership back to the agency: the Trade Partner/client marker remains. Evidence: trace `b1f31ed70fbdb2f92f66488da5bda6f3e510a0c9`, actions 87 and 91-93 ([finding](doa-findings/b1f31ed70fbdb2f92f66488da5bda6f3e510a0c9.json)).

10. **Organisation isolation prevents one organisation inheriting another organisation’s notifications.** A notification assigned to a client is not visible to unrelated organisations. In particular, other members of the agent’s own agency do not gain access merely because their colleague created it for a client; only the submitting agent’s explicit creator access survives. This agency-coworker consequence is a model rule derived from client ownership (rule 4), creator-specific access (rule 9), and the shared isolation boundary—not from a dedicated CHED-P or agency-coworker trace. Supporting evidence: trace `65343fb21a64286537e39551a3306b99058ec6e4`, actions 22, 29 and 34, confirms organisation-scoped branch data ([finding](doa-findings/65343fb21a64286537e39551a3306b99058ec6e4.json)); trace `5dd87b7fee11881f5706c26e17617e68b19247d1` in [`doa-findings/_discovery.md`](doa-findings/_discovery.md) confirms the converse notification boundary, where a client-organisation member cannot see a notification assigned to the agent’s own agency.

11. **Responsible-person/contact data auto-populate from the assigned organisation.** For client-assigned work, the responsible contact name, telephone, email and organisation come from the client, not the agent’s agency. The audit trail still identifies the agent as submitter. Evidence: trace `545412266caaec96b0aae12262fecfee3e099888`, actions 94-96 ([finding](doa-findings/545412266caaec96b0aae12262fecfee3e099888.json)).

12. **Importer data auto-populate from the assigned organisation.** The represented client supplies the importer identity, address, telephone and email. Changing the assigned organisation before submission must therefore recalculate or replace these organisation-derived defaults so they do not remain attached to the previous owner. Evidence: trace `545412266caaec96b0aae12262fecfee3e099888`, actions 97-98 ([finding](doa-findings/545412266caaec96b0aae12262fecfee3e099888.json)); the change-before-submit evidence is trace `851759a01b1ff318ca2a82fb278f9c41b05e9616` in [`doa-findings/_discovery.md`](doa-findings/_discovery.md).

## CHED-P application

For CHED-P, replace every Plant fixture organisation above with the authenticated user’s POAO organisation or an authorised POAO client organisation. Preserve the ownership, visibility, Trade Partner marking, pre-submit reassignment and assigned-organisation auto-population rules. Do not treat the Plant-specific labels (“plants importer or agency”, Plant Agency, Plant Organisation) as CHED-P copy.
