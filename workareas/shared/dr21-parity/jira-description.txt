h3. How it was produced

Both sides were captured as Playwright traces, mined into comparable page models using one shared extractor, diffed mechanically, and every finding was then adversarially verified against the captured artefacts.

* *Frontend side needed no capture harness.* Its own {{features}} Playwright project renders every page; the models were recovered from those traces. 264 tests, all green, 1.4 minutes.
* *Prototype side* needed a DR2.1 walker, written in Phase 1 and now living in the workspace. 26 specs, all green, 2.7 minutes — the repeatable command for refreshing the corpus when the designers move.
* *Corpus*: 103 page models (33 frontend, 70 prototype), 29 paired screens, 468 mechanical deltas, plus 4 frontend-only and 23 prototype-only screens.

h3. Quality controls that fired

* *93 deltas were deliberately not raised* as findings, each with a recorded reason, so the decision is visible rather than implicit.
* *60 of the 97 findings were materially corrected* by the verification stage — scope narrowed or claims amended against the source.
* *1 finding was refuted and dropped*: a prototype back link that exists only to escape the prototype chooser. A delta, not work.
* Two capture defects were caught and fixed before any diffing. Trace DOM dumps arrive JSON-encoded, which silently emptied every {{govuk-*}} class selector while leaving models looking healthy. And the prototype builds {{notification-hub}} and {{review-notification}} from bespoke {{app-*}} markup rather than govuk-frontend components, which would have read as "the prototype has no task list at all" on the two most important screens in the service. Both sides were re-captured after the fix.

h3. Prototype defects found, for the designers

Not frontend work — worth raising with the design team:

* {{renderDeleteNotificationPage}} hardcodes a {{design-release-2/}} view path, the only one in {{routes.js}}, so {{design-release-2.1/delete-notification.html}} is never rendered. The files are byte-identical today, so the DR2.1 copy could be edited indefinitely with no effect.
* {{view-template}}'s primary button reads "Save and continue" but the POST only writes the template name and redirects back to {{/templates}}.
* On a germinal-only consignment the per-panel change link still reads "Change number of animals" ({{changeCountLabel}} hardcoded).
